Same monkey, bigger repertoire

The prototype's monkey kept shape for shape, in the same 56 × 72 frame. Every animation below is CSS on those same parts, grouped by the moment on the site that triggers it.

Before · after

What changed

  • Hoodie with >_ instead of the starred T-shirt; sleeves end in small hands.
  • Warm fur, a messy swept mop, a pointed goatee, a slimmer body, a tail.
  • Red eyes whenever the glasses lift or fly off; the white dots on the lenses are their glow.
  • Two-part arms (shoulder, elbow), still plain rectangles, so he can smoke, wave, cross his arms or hammer.

Real size, in the hologram

Size: he is 72 px tall, as in the prototype; the drawing is vector, so a bigger monkey costs nothing.

The two special forms are drawn as tropes, not copies: a cape and a glowing >_ badge for the superhero, golden spiked hair, an aura and a charged beam for the rage. The beam is the hologram's own cyan: he wipes the page with its own light.
